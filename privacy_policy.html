Privacy Policy

TerraLive: Live Earth Camera · Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the TerraLive: Live Earth Camera app ("TerraLive", "the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it on iPhone or iPad. TerraLive does not require an account, and we do not run our own servers that collect your personal data. Most processing happens on your device.

2. Information handled by the App

2.1 Location

With your permission ("While Using the App"), TerraLive uses your device's location to:

  • show your position on the map and the distance to places, landmarks and your saved parking spot;
  • print your address, coordinates, altitude and time on GPS Map Camera photos;
  • power the compass, speedometer, coordinates finder and "Near Me" search;
  • show live flights in the area around you.

Your location is not sold, and it is not stored by us. When a feature needs it, a location or map area is sent to the service providers listed in Section 5 (for example, Apple for addresses and nearby search, OpenSky Network for flights in the visible map area).

2.2 Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you open the GPS Map Camera. Photos are processed on your device to add the location stamp. A photo is saved to your photo library only when you tap Save, using "add only" access; TerraLive cannot read the other photos in your library. When you choose a photo from your library to stamp, only that photo is shared with the App through the system photo picker. We do not upload your photos.

2.3 Information stored on your device

TerraLive stores a small amount of data locally on your device, such as your selected language, whether onboarding is complete, your saved parking spot, your World Clock cities, your preferred units and cached images. This data stays on your device and is deleted when you delete the App. You can clear cached images in Settings.

2.4 Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple provides us with the information needed to confirm whether you have an active subscription.

2.5 Support emails

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, the content of your message and any details you include (the App adds your app version to feedback emails). We use this only to respond to you.

3. How information is used

  • To provide the App's features described above.
  • To unlock and restore premium features you have purchased.
  • To answer your support requests.
  • To keep the App working correctly and improve it.

We do not use your information to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell personal information.

4. Legal bases

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we process information because it is necessary to provide the App you asked for (performance of a contract), based on your consent (for example, location and camera permissions, which you can withdraw at any time), or for our legitimate interests in operating and supporting the App.

6. Storage and retention

Data stored by the App stays on your device until you delete it or remove the App. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can allow or deny location, camera and photo access at any time in iOS Settings > TerraLive. Features that need a permission will not work without it.
  • You can clear your parking spot, World Clock cities and cached images inside the App, or delete the App to remove all local data.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to processing. Contact us at the email below to make a request.

8. Security

Connections to the services listed above use HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we keep the data we handle to a minimum.

9. Children

TerraLive is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has contacted us with personal information, please email us so we can delete it.

10. International transfers

Third-party services used by the App may process data in countries other than your own. They are responsible for protecting that data under their own policies and applicable law.

11. Accuracy of content

Live cameras, satellite and street imagery, flight data, addresses and GPS readings come from third parties and device sensors and may be delayed, incomplete or inaccurate. Satellite and street-level imagery is not live. Please see our Terms of Use.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
