Privacy Policy

Live Earth Cam: Wanderpost · Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the Live Earth Cam: Wanderpost app ("Wanderpost", "the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it on iPhone or iPad. Wanderpost does not require an account, and we do not run our own servers that store your personal data. Your postcards, passport, trips and settings are kept on your device. The App uses third-party services for maps, live video, advertising, analytics, crash reporting and subscriptions, as described below.

2. Information handled by the App

2.1 Location

With your permission ("While Using the App"), Wanderpost uses your device's location to:

  • show your position on the map, your town and local time on the Home screen, and the distance to places, landmarks, live cameras and your saved parking spot;
  • show the live cameras closest to you;
  • write the place name, address, coordinates and time on GPS Camera photos and on postcards you make with the camera;
  • power the compass, speedometer, trip history, coordinates finder and "Near Me" search;
  • show live flights in the area around you.

We do not sell your location and we do not store it on our servers. When a feature needs it, a location or map area is sent to the service providers listed in Section 5 (for example, Apple for addresses and nearby search, OpenSky Network for flights in the visible map area, or Google when you open Google Street View for a place).

2.2 Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you open the GPS Camera. Photos are processed on your device to add the location stamp. When you tap Catch this moment on a live camera, the App takes a picture of what is on your screen to make a postcard; this also stays on your device. A photo or postcard is saved to your photo library only when you tap Save, using "add only" access; Wanderpost cannot read the other photos in your library. When you choose a photo from your library, only that photo is shared with the App through the system photo picker. We do not upload your photos or postcards.

2.3 Information stored on your device

Wanderpost stores data locally on your device, such as your postcards and the notes you write on them, your passport stamps and streak, your planned trips and packing lists, saved speedometer trips, your saved parking spot, World Clock cities, selected language, units, reminder setting and cached images. This data stays on your device and is deleted when you delete the App. You can remove postcards and trips inside the App and clear cached images in Settings.

2.4 Advertising and tracking

The free version of Wanderpost shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, IDFA, if you allow tracking), approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads. On first launch iOS asks whether you allow tracking; if you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised using it. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), a consent form lets you choose how your data is used for ads. You can change tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Premium subscribers do not see ads.

2.5 Analytics, crash reports and session insights

To understand how the App is used and to fix problems, we use:

  • Firebase Analytics (Google): anonymous usage events such as screens opened and features used, plus device and app version information;
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google): crash reports with device state and app version at the time of the crash;
  • Firebase Remote Config (Google): app settings we can change without an update;
  • Microsoft Clarity: session replays and heatmaps showing how screens are used (taps, scrolling, screens visited) to improve the design. Clarity masks text you type.

This data is not linked to your name or email, and we do not use it to identify you.

2.6 Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple and our subscription provider, RevenueCat, give us the information needed to confirm whether you have an active subscription (an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history in the App).

2.7 Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App schedules a daily reminder on your device. Reminders are created locally; we do not send push notifications from a server and do not collect a push token for this.

2.8 Support emails

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, the content of your message and any details you include (the App adds your app version to feedback emails). We use this only to respond to you.

3. How information is used

  • To provide the App's features described above.
  • To show ads in the free version and measure their performance.
  • To unlock and restore premium features you have purchased.
  • To understand usage, fix crashes and improve the App.
  • To answer your support requests.

We do not sell personal information.

4. Legal bases

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we process information because it is necessary to provide the App you asked for (performance of a contract), based on your consent (for example location, camera and photo permissions, tracking and personalised ads, which you can withdraw at any time), or for our legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving the App.

6. Storage and retention

Data stored by the App stays on your device until you delete it or remove the App. Data collected by the third-party services above is kept according to their own retention policies (for example, Firebase Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months). Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can allow or deny location, camera, photo, tracking and notification access at any time in iOS Settings > Wanderpost.
  • You can turn the daily reminder off in the App's Settings.
  • You can remove postcards, trips, your parking spot, World Clock cities and cached images inside the App, or delete the App to remove all local data.
  • You can remove ads by subscribing to Wanderpost Premium.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to or restrict processing. Contact us at the email below to make a request.

8. Security

Connections to the services listed above use HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we keep the data we handle to a minimum.

9. Children

Wanderpost is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has contacted us with personal information, please email us so we can delete it.

10. International transfers

Third-party services used by the App may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. They are responsible for protecting that data under their own policies and applicable law.

11. Accuracy of content

Live cameras, local times, sky conditions, satellite and street imagery, flight data, addresses and GPS readings come from third parties and device sensors and may be delayed, incomplete or inaccurate. Live cameras are public streams owned by their broadcasters. Satellite and street-level imagery is not live. Please see our Terms of Use.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
